I am a Ph.D. candidate in mathematics at Nanjing University. My research concerns singularities of viscosity solutions of Hamilton–Jacobi equations and their connections with Lagrangian dynamics, differential geometry, and optimal transport. I am particularly interested in magnetic mechanical systems and in using geometric methods to study Hamiltonian dynamics.

Research

I study the structure and propagation of singularities, with a focus on the interplay between Hamilton–Jacobi equations, geometry, and dynamics. My work includes the Mather quotient problem, global propagation of singularities for magnetic mechanical systems, and singularity propagation in optimal transport.

My current interests include geometric descriptions of cut loci through touching from below, their stability under perturbations, and the construction of connecting orbits from singularities of weak KAM solutions. I also study singularities of distance functions in Finsler geometry.
